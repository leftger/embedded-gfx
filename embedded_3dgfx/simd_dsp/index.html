SIMD, DSP, and SWAR arithmetic primitives.

Provides fast zero-division color clamping, vector dot products, and packed channel operations with transparent acceleration on ARM Cortex-M4/M33 DSP (target_feature = "dsp") and portable fallback for Cortex-M0/M3/desktop.
