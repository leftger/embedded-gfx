Procedural 3D mesh generators for low-poly primitives.

Inspired by Bevy’s bevy_mesh::primitives::dim3, adapted for zero-allocation, fixed-capacity embedded execution. All shapes produce precomputed Geometry views suitable for crate::mesh::K3dMesh.
